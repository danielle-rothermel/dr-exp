## Throughput observations

Single job, nothing else on the GPU (`worker_concurrency: 2`, one job in
flight). Per-epoch seconds come from the `kind: "epoch"` records in
`metrics.jsonl`; round seconds from the `kind: "round"` records.

| Round | Subset size | Epochs | First epoch (s) | Min (s) | Median (s) | Mean (s) | Max (s) | Round total (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 16666 (`n_samples: 0.5` of the pool) | 22 | 5.40 | 2.87 | 3.02 | 3.13 | 5.40 | 68.8 |
| 1 | 33333 (full pool) | 33 | 7.39 | 5.78 | 6.11 | 6.18 | 7.39 | 203.8 |

- 55 epochs, 272.6 s of epoch time in total.
- Steady-state cost scales with subset size: ~3.0 s/epoch at 16666 samples and
  ~6.1 s/epoch at 33333, i.e. ~0.18 ms per sample per epoch on MPS in both
  rounds.
- The first epoch of each round is the outlier (5.40 s and 7.39 s against
  medians of 3.02 s and 6.11 s) — MPS warm-up and the round's dataloader
  construction.
- Job wall time from the dr-exec run record: 280.2 s
  (`2026-08-21T18:31:29.102077Z` → `2026-08-21T18:36:09.212757Z`), so ~7.6 s of
  the attempt sat outside epoch time (process spawn, imports, dataset load,
  final evaluation, teardown; `teardown_duration_ns: 25125`).
